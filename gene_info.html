National Identities & Symbols

National Flag

The National Flag of India, or Tiranga, is a horizontal tricolor of deep saffron (kesari) at the top, white in the middle, and dark green at the bottom in equal proportions. A navy blue 24-spoke wheel, the Ashoka Chakra, is centered on the white band. It was adopted on July 22, 1947.

National Anthem

"Jana Gana Mana" is the national anthem of India, composed by Nobel laureate Rabindranath Tagore in Bengali. It was officially adopted by the Constituent Assembly on January 24, 1950. The full version takes approximately 52 seconds to perform and signifies India's unity, diversity, and sovereignty.

National Song

The national song of India is "Vande Mataram," composed in Sanskrit by Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay. It was officially adopted by the Constituent Assembly of India on January 24, 1950, and holds equal status with the National Anthem, "Jana Gana Mana," having inspired the nation's freedom struggle.

National Animal

The national animal of India is the Bengal Tiger (Panthera tigris tigris), adopted in 1972 to symbolize India's grace, strength, agility, and power. Known for its thick yellow coat and dark stripes, the tiger replaced the lion as the national animal due to its widespread presence across the country.

National Bird

The national bird of India is the Indian Peacock (Pavo cristatus), designated in 1963 due to its widespread presence across the country, cultural significance, and rich representation in Indian art and mythology. It is a large, colorful bird, with the male displaying vibrant blue plumage and a long, fan-like tail.

National Plants

The national tree of India is the Banyan tree, symbolizing immortality and unity due to its vast, spreading roots. Other official national botanical symbols include the Lotus as the national flower and the Mango as the national fruit.
