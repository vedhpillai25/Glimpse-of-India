India

India, officially the Republic of India, is a country of South Asia. It is the 7th largest country by area; the most populous country; and the most populous democracy.

India shares borders with Pakistan, China, Nepal, Bhutan, Bangladesh, Myanmar, Sri Lanka, Maldives, Thailand, and Indonesia.
